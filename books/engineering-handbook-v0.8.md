# Volume 11 — Price Comparison, Unit Price and Promotion Engine

The platform compares effective unit prices rather than shelf prices alone.

Quantities are normalized into compatible dimensions, promotions are modeled with their conditions and anomalous prices are validated before recommendation.

Exact product matches receive the strongest comparison confidence.

Every ranking explains unit price, promotion requirements, freshness and uncertainty.
