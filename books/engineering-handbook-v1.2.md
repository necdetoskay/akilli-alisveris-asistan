# Volume 15 — Observability, Operations, SLO and Incident Management

The platform monitors technical health, data freshness, matching quality, pricing correctness and user-visible outcomes.

SLOs are based on user experience rather than server uptime alone.

Data-quality regressions may trigger operational incidents.

Every major failure path has dashboards, alerts, runbooks, recovery checks and postmortem requirements.
