# Volume 16 — Security, Privacy, Threat Modeling and Compliance Foundations

The platform uses defense in depth across clients, APIs, application services, workers, databases and scrapers.

Security protects confidentiality, availability and price-data integrity.

Authorization is enforced server-side, secrets remain isolated and untrusted retailer content runs within restricted scraper boundaries.

Secure development, audit records and security incident handling are mandatory parts of delivery.
