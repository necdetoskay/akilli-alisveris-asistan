# Volume 20 — Implementation Roadmap, Sprint Plan and Production Readiness

The platform will be implemented through risk-first vertical slices.

The first working flow covers one retailer and one category from ingestion through user-visible comparison.

Each sprint must produce tested, observable and demonstrable software.

Initial production remains deliberately narrow until data quality, matching precision and operational stability are proven.
