# Volume 10 — Product Normalization, Matching and Confidence Engine

Retailer listings are normalized into typed product facts.

Candidate generation combines identifiers, lexical search, semantic vectors and structured filters.

Final decisions use explainable evidence, category-aware confidence scores and hard-conflict rules.

High-confidence automatic matching is allowed only when no critical contradiction exists.

Uncertain cases enter manual review, and every decision remains reproducible and versioned.
