# Promotion and Discount Modeling

## Promotion types

```text
direct discount
percentage discount
loyalty-card price
coupon discount
multi-buy
buy-one-get-one
minimum basket discount
bundle promotion
cashback
free delivery
```

## Promotion record

Suggested fields:

```text
promotion_type
headline
conditions
required_quantity
required_membership
required_coupon
minimum_basket_amount
discount_amount
discount_percentage
valid_from
valid_until
confidence
source_text
```

## Examples

```text
2 al 1 öde
3 adet 250 TL
Money Kart ile 89,90 TL
500 TL üzeri sepette 50 TL indirim
```

## Rule

A conditional promotion must not be presented as the unconditional shelf price.
