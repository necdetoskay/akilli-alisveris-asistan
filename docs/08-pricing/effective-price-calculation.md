# Effective Price Calculation

## Effective price

The effective price represents the amount attributable to the compared quantity after valid promotion conditions are applied.

## Examples

### Multi-buy

```text
3 adet 250 TL
effective price per item = 83,33 TL
```

### Buy one get one

```text
2 al 1 öde
displayed item price = 120,00 TL
effective price for 2 items = 120,00 TL
effective unit price = 60,00 TL/adet
```

### Loyalty price

```text
normal price = 109,90 TL
loyalty price = 89,90 TL
```

The system must show both and label the membership requirement.

## Costs that may affect effective price

Depending on product scope:

- mandatory delivery fee,
- service fee,
- minimum order threshold,
- membership cost,
- refundable deposit.

These should not be silently folded into the item price without explanation.
