# Incident Response Lifecycle

## Lifecycle

```text
detect
    ↓
acknowledge
    ↓
triage
    ↓
contain
    ↓
mitigate
    ↓
recover
    ↓
verify
    ↓
review
```

## Severity examples

### SEV-1

Broad outage, data corruption, security exposure or incorrect prices shown at large scale.

### SEV-2

Major retailer unavailable, degraded search or optimizer, or alert delivery substantially delayed.

### SEV-3

Limited component degradation with a workaround and no broad user impact.
