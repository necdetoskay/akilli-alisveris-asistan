# Recommendation Strategies

## Lowest total cost

Returns the mathematically cheapest valid basket.

## Single-retailer convenience

Prioritizes one retailer even when the total is slightly higher.

## Balanced recommendation

Balances:

- total cost,
- retailer count,
- data freshness,
- match confidence,
- promotion complexity,
- substitutions.

## Preferred retailer

Uses the user's preferred retailer when the cost difference is within a configurable tolerance.

## No-membership plan

Excludes loyalty-card and paid-membership prices.

## Immediate availability

Prioritizes offers available now over cheaper but uncertain offers.
